# Around Town local social event discovery

Use public social posts to find local happenings that general news searches miss. Recheck sources for every edition; this is a discovery seed list, not a permanent event schedule.

## Search and verification

- Search combinations of city, activity and target month/year: `Boca Raton cars coffee October 2026`, `Delray Beach pop up this weekend`, `West Palm Beach live music [date]`.
- Run focused `site:instagram.com` and `site:facebook.com` searches using city/activity/date and discovered organizer names. Search an exact event title to locate its official announcement.
- Follow organizer/venue website links to establish the correct social account. Check accessible recent event posts for date, special theme, admission, parking and cancellation changes. Do not mistake posting date for event date.
- Include public community recommendations as leads; verify with the host. Use South Florida on the Cheap, FLA Car Shows, CarCruiseFinder and other local calendars for discovery/corroboration. Do not trust automatically generated recurrence lists.
- Retain internally: event identity, occurrence date/time with America/New_York timezone, venue/address, verified costs, organizer URL, discovery URL, verification time and material conflicts. Resolve conflicts with the most recent specific organizer notice; omit disputed details if unresolved.
- Search cancellation/postponement/weather updates immediately before publication. Avoid expired events and prior-year flyers. If an essential date or venue cannot be established, skip the event.
- Rank by proximity, usefulness, novelty and source quality. Do not inflate prominence from follower counts or generic aggregator hype. Include car culture alongside music, markets, arts, outdoor, food and seasonal happenings.
