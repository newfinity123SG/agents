# Editorial Layout Reference — Palm Beach Times v6

## Design goal
Create a calm, premium local broadsheet. The page should look edited, not packed. Local identity, readability, and visual hierarchy outrank density.

## Canonical visible regions
1. Graphical masthead
2. Today’s Thread
3. Front Page lead + Morning in 60 Seconds
4. Weather & Ocean
5. Money + Markets at a Glance
6. Around Town: Boca · Delray · West Palm
7. Development & Deals
8. Palm Beach County & South Florida News
9. Community Issues
10. Technology & Community
11. World & Lifestyle
12. Morning Strip
13. Screen Guide: TV, Streaming & Movies
14. Quick Extras

Use `assets/newspaper-template.html` as the structural skeleton. Do not recreate a tiled dashboard.

## Weekday density ceilings
- 1 Front Page lead
- exactly 4 Morning in 60 Seconds briefs
- up to 2 Money stories
- up to 3 Around Town stories
- up to 2 Development & Deals items
- up to 4 Palm Beach County & South Florida News stories
- 1 Community Issues main item + optional short second update
- up to 2 Technology & Community stories
- up to 2 World & Lifestyle stories
- Screen Guide: 2-4 TV/streaming + 1-2 theatrical
- up to 3 Quick Extras

Do not force quotas. Use `Quiet this morning` when a required desk has no meaningful fresh item.

## Duplicate control
A core event/entity gets one full visible story. Brief cross-references are allowed, but never duplicate the full treatment across desks.

## Masthead
Use the bundled graphical Palm Beach Times artwork. Keep it centered, proportional, fully visible and self-contained. Never replace it with a text-only nameplate in normal production.

## Money
Start with the unified Markets at a Glance feature containing S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq 1-day intraday rows for the most recent active or completed trading session. Use dense raw price-and-volume observations: a sharp unsmoothed line, previous-close reference, compact volume bars and absolute point change with an up/down arrow rather than a percentage. Show the actual session date when markets are closed, including weekends, holidays, and premarket mornings. Then run at most two strong Money stories on weekdays. Weekend editions do not force routine mortgage-rate recaps.

## Local desks
### Around Town
Up to 3 neighborhood-scale items. Boca/Delray first, then West Palm.

### Development & Deals
Up to 2 strong development/deal items. Use status labels when relevant.

### Palm Beach County & South Florida News
Up to 4 consequential local items, Palm Beach County first.

### Community Issues
One main ongoing issue plus a short secondary update when useful. State What’s happening, Why people care, What happens next.

## Support desks
### Technology & Community
Up to 2 meaningful technology/community items.

### World & Lifestyle
Up to 2 strong items across consequential world/national news, travel, sports, outdoors, food and culture.

## Screen Guide
Normal weekday: 2-4 TV/streaming + 1-2 theatrical.
Friday/weekend: 4-6 TV/streaming + 2-3 theatrical.
Keep it current, concise and release-focused. Celebrity/industry chatter may appear only as a small note inside the Screen Guide.

## Saturday Weekend Edition
Saturday should feel purpose-built for the weekend. Give more visual/editorial weight to fresh local news, things to do, restaurants, food, culture, beach/ocean, sports/outdoors, travel and Screen Guide. Reduce repetitive mortgage/rate material and generic market filler.

## Sunday Week Ahead Edition
Sunday is not a refreshed Saturday layout. Treat unchanged Saturday stories as stale by default and shift the editorial center forward into the coming Monday-Sunday period. Give prominent weight to upcoming local meetings/events, municipal and development decisions, economic/market catalysts, mortgage/housing/insurance developments, technology launches, sports, entertainment premieres, travel disruptions, and weather/ocean risks. Carry Saturday material only when something materially changed or when it is essential context for a dated week-ahead item, and rewrite it around what changed or what happens next.

## Friday edition
Friday may add Weekend Outlook emphasis and slightly more leisure/event coverage while preserving the same grouped layout.

## Navigation
Maximum six links:
**Front Page · Local · Money · Technology · Lifestyle · Comic**

## Visual rules
- Open columns separated by thin vertical rules.
- Double horizontal rules between major bands.
- No border around every desk or every story.
- No filler to equalize column heights.
- No typography shrinking to cram content.
- White space is intentional.
- Keep body copy comfortably readable.

## Mobile
Collapse grouped desks into one reading column while preserving hierarchy, graphical masthead legibility and spacing.
