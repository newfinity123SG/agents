# Daily Comic Rotation

## Goal
Make every edition's comic visibly and conceptually fresh. The reader should never feel that the same weather joke or beach character was merely redressed.

## Daily selection
After the news research is complete:

1. Pick one **safe comic theme** from today's paper or ordinary South Florida life.
2. Use the local calendar date to force variety. Compute the day-of-year modulo 8 and use the matching starting lane below unless today's news strongly supports a different lane.
3. Check the final idea against the anti-repetition rules before rendering.

### Rotation lanes
0. Beach / ocean / boating
1. Boca / Delray / West Palm around-town life
2. Mortgage / housing / real-estate absurdities
3. Traffic / commuting / airports / travel
4. AI / technology / gadgets
5. Food / restaurants / shopping / weekend plans
6. Markets / business / office life
7. Weather / Florida seasons / tropical living

## Rotate the cast
Do not default to the same beachgoer. Rotate among original fictional characters and visual viewpoints, for example:
- a couple or family
- a retiree or snowbird
- a local business owner
- a mortgage broker archetype
- an office worker
- a surfer, boater, golfer, cyclist, or dog walker
- a pelican, ibis, lizard, or other anthropomorphic Florida creature
- an AI assistant represented as a fictional device or robot
- a group scene with no recurring protagonist

Never portray a real private person without an explicit request and usable reference image.

## Rotate the joke structure
Vary among:
- setup / surprise / punchline
- escalating three-panel problem
- visual misunderstanding
- mock headline or sign gag
- expectation versus reality
- before / during / after
- straight character plus absurd character
- silent visual punchline with minimal dialogue

## Rotate visual treatment
Keep the overall vintage-newspaper family, but vary details such as:
- mostly black-and-white ink
- muted two-color halftone
- Sunday-comic-inspired restrained color
- heavier crosshatching
- cleaner mid-century newspaper line art

Do not imitate a named artist or existing strip.

## Anti-repetition checks
Before rendering, confirm:
- the main setting is not the same as the immediately prior edition when prior context is available;
- the main character archetype is not the same as the immediately prior edition when prior context is available;
- the punchline is not another version of the prior day's joke;
- the same exact weather-alert / umbrella gag is not reused within 30 days when prior context is available;
- the comic title is new for the day;
- dialogue stays short enough to read on a phone.

If prior comic context is unavailable, use the date-based lane plus a fresh daily headline/topic to create deterministic variety rather than choosing the same default concept.
